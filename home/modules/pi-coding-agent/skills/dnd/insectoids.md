## Insectoids
> These giant insect-like creatures live underground and burrow up to attack when attracted by noise or pheromone signals. Originally from the planet Sorne, interstellar entrepreneurs managed to capture, genetically-modify, and vat-grow the insect colonies for use as weapons. It's not clear who placed Sorne insects on this planet, but they are here and as dangerous as ever.
> 
> — Faction description
**Insectoids** are an ecosystem of genetically engineered insect-like [animals](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Animals "Animals") that function, in part, like a permanently hostile [faction](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Faction "Faction"). The insectoids that populate this faction are animals that are not always automatically hostile to colonists, but can be a serious surprise threat to any colony when encountered during an [infestation](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Infestation "Infestation").

## Summary
Insectoids are [animals](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Animal "Animal"). Uniquely, insectoids experience [hypothermic slowdown](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Hypothermic_slowdown "Hypothermic slowdown") instead of [hypothermia](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Hypothermia "Hypothermia"). They have 100% [Toxic Resistance](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Toxic_Resistance "Toxic Resistance") along with [Vacuum Resistance](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Vacuum_Resistance "Vacuum Resistance"), rendering them immune to [toxic buildup](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Toxic_buildup "Toxic buildup") and [vacuum exposure](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Ailments#Vacuum_exposure "Ailments"). When butchered, they give [insect meat](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Insect_meat "Insect meat"), which is disgusting for humans to eat. Insectoids may be tamed, but as most spawn hostile, it is a very difficult task - often relying on the [bond](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Bond "Bond") chance whenever you tend to them.
### Infestation
As part of an [infestation](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Infestation "Infestation"), they are a "faction" that is hostile to every other faction in the world. They will maintain [hives](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Hive "Hive"), which, if left untampered, will produce more insectoids - as well as [insect jelly](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Insect_jelly "Insect jelly"). Unlike other raiders, and because they are animals, insectoids don't have any ranged attacks. However, their decent armor and sheer numbers make them extreme threats if not handled properly.
Infestations will normally stay within a 10-tile radius from their hive. Insectoids are "triggered" when any of them are downed or killed, for any reason.<sup>&#91;Detail needed&#93;</sup> Insectoids will mine through walls and rock anywhere they roam around, even to the point of ceiling collapses.
The Too Deep: Infestation [event](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Event "Event"), caused by using [deep drills](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Deep_drill "Deep drill"), creates hostile insectoids but without hives.
### Wild Insectoids
[Megascarabs](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Megascarab "Megascarab") may be found in the wild in the desert or extreme desert [biomes](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Biome "Biome"), where they are not hostile until attacked. Other insectoid types are not found naturally. If a hostile insect is [downed](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Downed "Downed") and has no hive, they will become neutral.
### Structures
Hives can spawn as part of [ancient shrines](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Ancient_shrine "Ancient shrine"), only becoming active when the area is revealed, non-hostile [megascarabs](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Megascarab "Megascarab") can be stored in [ancient cryptosleep caskets](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Ancient_cryptosleep_casket "Ancient cryptosleep casket"). For [Stockpiles](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Landmarks#Stockpile "Landmarks") Insectoids can also be found inside rooms apart from the exit room, unlike ancient shrines their hives are dormant, and incapable of producing more hives. [Infested settlements](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Landmarks#Infested_settlement "Landmarks") will also contain dormant hives along with Insectoids. ## Lore
See also: [Lore](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Lore "Lore")
Only a limited amount is known as about the origins of Insectoids, and most is provided from the faction description above. The planet Sorne was the original homeworld of the [insectoids](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Insectoid "Insectoid"), before they were captured, genetically modified, and [vat-grown](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Lore#Technologies_and_Militaries "Lore") by interstellar entrepreneurs for use as weapons, and exported to other worlds by parties unknown. As all seemingly-alien life is claimed to have originated on Earth,[^2] it is possible that the original pre-weaponization Sorne Geneline evolved from Earth life on the planet, or were already genetically engineered for some reason before being modified again.[^3]
The purpose of the modification is known however - they were intended to act as artificial ecosystem of insectoids designed to fight mechanoid invasions.[^4] Given the past tense used in the source, it is possible that the current insectoid ecosystem seen on the rimworld does not function as originally designed. Notably however, [mechanoids](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Mechanoids "Mechanoids") and insectoids do remain permanently hostile to each other, a fact that can occasionally be exploited.
