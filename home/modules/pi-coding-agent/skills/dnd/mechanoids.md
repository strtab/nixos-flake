**Mechanoids** are deadly autonomously intelligent robots.
## Summary
Mechanoids are machines. They do not eat, do not feel [mood](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Mood "Mood") or [pain](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Pain "Pain"), and are immune to [fire](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Fire "Fire"), [toxic buildup](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Toxic_buildup "Toxic buildup"), and temperature extremes - despite having a Comfortable Temperature range. Enemy mechanoids have no needs at all. As machines, they are vulnerable to [EMP](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/EMP "EMP") attacks, though will "Adapt" for 2,200 [ticks](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Time "Time") (36.67 secs) (regardless of source), making them immune to all further EMP strikes.
Enemy mechanoids will automatically die if they are [downed](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Downed "Downed") through damage.
### Mechanoid Hive
Main article: [Mechanoid Hive](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Mechanoid_Hive "Mechanoid Hive")
Hostile mechanoids are part of a **Mechanoid Hive**. They can be found sealed in ancient ruins in all biomes, which spawn inside mountains or outside on the landscape. They may also [raid](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Raid "Raid") the player's base directly, or be found in most [poison ships](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Crashed_Ship_Part#Poison_Ship "Crashed Ship Part"), [psychic ships](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Crashed_Ship_Part#Psychic_Ship "Crashed Ship Part"), and [mech clusters](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Mech_cluster "Mech cluster"). They may sometimes appear dormant in Ancient Complex and guarding any relic with [Ideology](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Ideology "Ideology") DLC.
### Allied Mechanoids
Main article: [Mechanitor](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Mechanitor "Mechanitor")
In the [Biotech DLC](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Biotech_DLC "Biotech DLC"), many more mechanoid types are added. You can create friendly mechanoids by [gestating](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Mechanitor#Acquisition "Mechanitor") them. A [mechanitor](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Mechanitor "Mechanitor") with enough [bandwidth](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Bandwidth "Bandwidth") is required to both create them, and keep mechs from going feral. The Biotech DLC must be active for you to create mechanoids.
These gestated variants require [power](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Power "Power") and have to be recharged, which creates [Pollution](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Pollution "Pollution"). Friendly mechanoids can be repaired with power and no other cost. As long as they leave a [corpse](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Corpse "Corpse"), friendly mechanoids can be resurrected for a [steel](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Steel "Steel") cost.
Allied mechanoids do not consume any power while in a caravan and will return to their previous work mode when they arrive at the destination.

## Lore
See also: [Lore](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Lore "Lore")
> *"Killer machines of unknown origin. Hidden in ancient structures, under mounds of dust, or at the bottom of the ocean, mechanoids can self-maintain for thousands of years. This group of mechs seems to be unified in purpose, but not well-coordinated in action. While local scholars believe they're autonomous weapons left over from an ancient war, tribal legends describe them as the demonic servants of a sleeping god."* - Faction description
In the [lore](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Lore "Lore"), Mechanoids are autonomous intelligent robots built for a variety of purposes. The technologies required to build them vary based on the complexity of the resultant mechanoid, with [Spacer-level](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Lore#Tech_levels "Lore")[^1] urbworlds[^2] being able to construct more simple mechanoids for companionship, combat and labor use, while only glitterworlds are capable of constructing the more advanced, quasi-conscious versions.[^1] Others still are constructed and utilized by [archotechs](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Lore#Archotech "Lore"). [^3][^4]
In-game, mechanoids can be constructed and controlled with a [mechlink](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Mechlink "Mechlink"). Otherwise, only combatant mechanoids from a single, somewhat mysterious source are seen. There are some indications that the Mechanoids seen in-game may have been built by, or otherwise allied with, an ancient army, perhaps that of the [Ancients](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Ancients "Ancients") faction.[^5]. Whatever the case however, they have since lost any allegiances they may have had and are now hostile to every other faction.
[Defoliator ship parts](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Crashed_ship_parts "Crashed ship parts") are usually associated with orbital-drop mechanoid armies.[^6]
[Insectoid](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Insectoid "Insectoid") ecosystems were genetically engineered to fight Mechanoid invasions[^7] and thus the two factions are always hostile to one another.
In addition to the existing mechanoid types encountered by a colony, the ancient exostrider ruins are described as being mechanoids before their destruction.

## Trivia
- Although all mechanoid-only weapons are 'Spacer' in terms of tech level, the hidden mechanoid faction itself is actually 'Ultra' tech level - one above 'Spacer', and the second highest tech level in the game, below 'Transcendent'.
- All mechanoids, except termites, have a 'clean' and an 'ancient' version. A mechanoid will turn ancient after 100 biological years, going from having a white hue to a faded light green hue, but the difference is purely cosmetic.
- Mechanoids can be downed by a psychic shock lance or acid spew ability; If a downed hostile mech regains the ability to move, they will just wander around without attacking anyone.
